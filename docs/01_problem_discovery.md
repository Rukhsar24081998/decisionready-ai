# Problem Discovery

## Project

**DecisionReady AI**  
*AI Decision Intelligence for Modern Hiring*

---

## Background

Modern Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) have significantly improved candidate sourcing, application tracking, interview scheduling, and hiring pipeline management. However, one of the most critical stages of recruitment—the final hiring decision—remains largely manual.

After multiple interview rounds, recruiters and hiring managers must review resumes, interview scorecards, qualitative feedback, and interviewer notes before deciding whether a candidate should proceed, be rejected, or undergo further evaluation.

While ATS platforms effectively collect and organize hiring data, they primarily function as systems of record. Recruiters are still responsible for manually synthesizing fragmented feedback, identifying conflicting opinions, and determining whether enough evidence exists to make a confident hiring decision.

---

## Problem Statement

Recruiters and hiring managers struggle to make timely and consistent hiring decisions because interview feedback is fragmented across multiple interviewers, scorecards, and qualitative notes.

Each interviewer evaluates different competencies, uses different evaluation styles, and provides feedback in varying levels of detail. As a result, recruiters spend considerable time manually reviewing interview notes, resolving conflicting opinions, and identifying gaps before facilitating hiring discussions.

This manual process increases recruiter effort, delays hiring decisions, introduces inconsistency in candidate evaluation, and increases the risk of making decisions based on incomplete evidence.

The problem is not a lack of hiring data. The problem is a lack of intelligence that helps recruiters understand whether the available evidence is sufficient to make a confident hiring decision.

---

## Target Users

### Primary User

**Technical Recruiter**

Responsible for:

- Managing hiring pipelines
- Coordinating interviews
- Collecting interviewer feedback
- Facilitating hiring discussions
- Driving final hiring decisions

**Pain points**

- Interview feedback is scattered across multiple scorecards and notes.
- Every interviewer evaluates candidates differently.
- Recruiters manually compare feedback before every hiring discussion.
- It is difficult to determine whether all required competencies have been evaluated.

### Secondary User

**Hiring Manager**

Responsible for making the final hiring decision.

Needs:

- A clear understanding of candidate strengths and weaknesses
- Confidence that all critical competencies have been evaluated
- Faster, evidence-backed hiring decisions

---

## Current Bottleneck

The largest bottleneck occurs after interviews are completed.

Although interview data is available, recruiters must manually answer questions such as:

- Have all required competencies been assessed?
- Do interviewers agree or disagree?
- Are there conflicting opinions?
- Is additional evidence required?
- Can the hiring team confidently make a decision?

Current ATS platforms provide the data but leave these questions unanswered.

---

## Opportunity

Instead of collecting more hiring data, recruiters need an intelligent layer that helps them interpret the evidence they already have.

This creates an opportunity for AI to move beyond simple summarization and provide structured decision support during the most critical stage of the hiring process.

---

## Proposed Solution

**DecisionReady AI** is an AI-powered **Decision Intelligence Layer** designed to work alongside existing Applicant Tracking Systems.

Rather than replacing ATS platforms, it analyzes existing hiring evidence—including the job description, candidate resume, interview scorecards, and interviewer feedback—to determine whether the hiring team has sufficient, reliable, and complete evidence to make a hiring decision.

The system performs structured reasoning by:

- Extracting required competencies from the job description
- Mapping interview evidence to each competency
- Detecting conflicting interviewer opinions
- Identifying competencies with insufficient evidence
- Measuring overall decision readiness
- Recommending the next best hiring action

Instead of answering:

> **"Who should we hire?"**

DecisionReady AI answers:

> **"Do we have enough evidence to make a fair and confident hiring decision?"**

---

## Product Vision

DecisionReady AI transforms interview feedback from static records into actionable hiring intelligence.

By acting as an AI-powered decision support layer, it helps recruiters and hiring managers reduce manual effort, improve evaluation consistency, and make faster, evidence-backed hiring decisions—without replacing their existing ATS.

---

## Why This Matters

The future of recruiting is not about collecting more information—it is about making better decisions from the information already available.

DecisionReady AI enables hiring teams to move from **data collection** to **decision intelligence**, helping organizations make more consistent, transparent, and evidence-driven hiring decisions.
